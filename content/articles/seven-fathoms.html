In an exclusive interview with CanadianSME Small Business Magazine, Courtney Howell and Terrence Howell, co-founders of 7 Fathoms Skin Care, share how a personal search for skin relief inspired a seaweed-based skincare business rooted in rural Newfoundland and Labrador.

Courtney and Terrence Howell are the co-founders of 7 Fathoms Skin Care, a Newfoundland and Labrador-based company producing proprietary North Atlantic seaweed extracts and barrier-supportive skincare products. What started with a simple question, whether seaweed could help, has grown into a company that harvests, extracts, formulates and manufactures in rural Newfoundland and Labrador.

7 Fathoms began with one very personal search for relief. How did that experience shape the way you see your customers today?

In many ways, we see our customers as fellow travellers on a similar journey.

When Terrence developed severe skin irritation and contact dermatitis associated with living with an ileostomy, we experienced firsthand the frustration and uncertainty that can come with searching for relief. We understand what it feels like to keep looking for something that might finally help.

As we learned ourselves, skin challenges rarely affect just one person. A spouse worries. A parent searches for answers. A caregiver invests time and energy trying to help someone they love feel more comfortable. That is the inspiration behind our brand promise: for skin that has been through storms, and those who care for it.

How did you go from a moment on the beach to a vertically integrated seaweed skincare company?

Our journey is one of curiosity, persistence and validation.

After seeing the impact that raw seaweed extract had on Terrence’s skin, our first goal wasn’t to build a company. It was simply to preserve and use it ourselves. We developed a gentle extraction process to protect the seaweed’s natural bioactive compounds and began incorporating the extracts into products used within our tourism business in Grates Cove.

A pivotal moment came when retired analytical chemist Dr. Robert Helleur became our Scientific Advisor and connected us with researchers at Memorial University. Later, Bounce Health Innovation introduced us to the BIO International Convention, which led to our acceptance into Emergence, the PEI BioAlliance’s bioscience incubator, and opportunities with the National Research Council.

What makes Newfoundland seaweed uniquely suited for skincare?

The Laminaria digitata and Fucus vesiculosus we harvest have spent millions of years adapting to the cold, turbulent waters off Newfoundland’s coast. To survive in that environment, they produce a diverse range of bioactive compounds, including fucoidan, laminarin and polyphenols.

Through collaborations with Memorial University and the Marine Institute, we have demonstrated antioxidant, anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial activity in our extracts. Holland College has also identified promising wound closure activity and UV absorption capabilities.

What has it been like building a marine biotechnology business in rural Newfoundland and Labrador?

We see operating in rural Newfoundland and Labrador as both an opportunity and a responsibility. Being located at the source of our primary ingredient allows us to remain closely connected to the seaweed, the ocean and the ecosystems that support our business.

Sustainability is embedded in our decisions. We work closely with DFO, monitor regrowth, respect fallow periods and maximize our extraction process to minimize harvest volumes. Our goal is to support both healthy coastal ecosystems and healthy rural communities.

What message would you share with small and medium-sized businesses?

Small and medium-sized businesses shouldn’t underestimate the power of place and community. Rural locations aren’t a disadvantage. Our experience has been the opposite. The seaweed, the coastline, the community and the people of Newfoundland are not obstacles to our business. They are the foundation of it.

Some of the most innovative ideas in the world begin with a simple question and a deep connection to where you come from.
