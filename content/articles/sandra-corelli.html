One goal has guided Sandra Corelli’s career as the founder of Humanicity Consulting Group and Humanicity Psychotherapy: creating a more human world of work.

Working at the intersection of leadership, workplace culture, and mental health, Sandra brings more than two decades of corporate leadership experience, along with her roles as a Registered Psychotherapist, executive coach, keynote speaker, and leadership and well-being strategist.

From corporate HR leader to Humanicity founder

Before founding Humanicity, Sandra spent more than 20 years in global leadership, human resources and workplace culture. She held executive and senior HR roles across Canadian organizations, leading large-scale culture and transformation initiatives.

Over time, she saw more clearly how deeply leadership, culture, and well-being shape both the employee experience and business performance. That insight became the foundation for Humanicity Consulting Group.

Where leadership meets mental health

Humanicity Consulting Group helps organizations create the conditions for both people and performance to thrive. Through tailored programs it provides:

  • Leadership development focused on self-leadership, presence, influence, accountability, and leading through change
  • Culture strategies that embed psychological safety, inclusion, trust, and well-being into everyday leadership
  • Keynotes and workshops on resilience, emotional intelligence, burnout prevention, workplace mental health, and human-centred leadership
  • Executive coaching for senior and emerging leaders

What differentiates Sandra’s approach is the combination of organizational experience and psychological insight. She draws from psychology, neuroscience, emotional intelligence, and two decades of corporate experience to turn complex ideas into practical strategies.

Supporting people through life’s challenges

As a Registered Psychotherapist, Sandra works with adults navigating anxiety, stress, burnout, grief and loss, trauma, health diagnoses, relationship challenges, workplace difficulties, and other life transitions. For her, these two areas of work are not separate worlds: both are about helping people navigate challenge and move forward with intention.

Advocacy and community

Sandra is an advocate for mental health, women’s advancement, and inclusive leadership. She is writing her first book, to be published in spring 2027, on navigating life’s unexpected challenges with agency, resilience, and self-compassion. She has served as HR Director-at-Large on the National Board of The Duke of Edinburgh’s International Award and supported women leaders through Fora: Network for Change and the Forum for Women Entrepreneurs.

What SMEs can learn

  • Human-centred culture: workplaces where people feel safe to contribute ideas, raise concerns, and learn from mistakes.
  • Emotional intelligence: leaders who understand their own emotions and respond to the needs of others.
  • Sustainable performance: practices that protect energy and capacity instead of asking people to cope with more.
  • Leading through change: understanding both the business and human sides of transformation.

Through Humanicity, Sandra demonstrates that when organizations put people at the heart of how they lead, both individuals and businesses are better positioned to thrive.
