    What’s Due Next

    The federal filing and remittance dates most small businesses face. Your own dates depend on your fiscal year end and filing frequency.

    CRA remittance rules →

    Estimate payroll deductions →

    When a due date falls on a weekend or public holiday, the CRA accepts payment on the next business day. Dates shown are already adjusted.

    1. Nov 2Q3 GST/HST return and payment dueQuarterly filers with a 30 September quarter end
    2. Nov 16October payroll remittance dueRegular remitters — CPP, EI and income tax withheld
    3. Dec 15November payroll remittance dueRegular remitters — CPP, EI and income tax withheld
    4. Dec 31Corporate tax instalmentCorporations paying monthly or quarterly instalments
    5. Dec 31Last day to incur deductible expensesBusinesses with a 31 December fiscal year end
    6. Jan 15December payroll remittance dueRegular remitters — CPP, EI and income tax withheld
    7. Feb 1Q4 GST/HST return and payment dueQuarterly filers with a 31 December quarter end
    8. Mar 1T4 and T5 slips filed and given to recipientsEvery employer — late penalties start at $100
    9. Mar 1RRSP contribution deadlineOwners taking salary who contribute personally
    10. Apr 30Personal tax balance owing dueSole proprietors and partners pay by 30 April, even though filing is due 15 June
    11. Jun 15T1 return due for self-employedSole proprietors and partners — balance owing was due 30 April
